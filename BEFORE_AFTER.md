# 📊 Before & After Comparison

## Visual & UX Improvements

### 🌌 Background & Atmosphere

**BEFORE:**
- 5000+ bright, neon stars
- Fast rotation (speed: 1.0)
- Multiple colored layers (purple, cyan)
- Bright nebula effects (5% opacity)
- Distracting motion

**AFTER:**
- 2500-3000 subtle stars
- Slow rotation (speed: 0.3)
- Single muted color (#7886a8)
- Minimal ambient glow (2% opacity)
- Calming, professional background

**Impact:** Background no longer competes with content for attention

---

### 🎨 3D Hero Animation

**BEFORE:**
- Rotation speed: 0.1, 0.15
- Float intensity: 1.5
- 30 sparkles at 0.3 opacity
- Auto-rotate: 0.5
- Bright, flashy

**AFTER:**
- Rotation speed: 0.05, 0.08 (50% slower)
- Float intensity: 0.8 (47% less)
- 15 sparkles at 0.2 opacity (50% less)
- Auto-rotate: 0.3 (40% slower)
- Elegant, refined

**Impact:** Animations are purposeful, not dizzying
